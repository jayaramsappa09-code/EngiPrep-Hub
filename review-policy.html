Academic Peer Review Policy

Last updated & audited: June 2026

1. Peer Oversight Obligation

To guarantee that our supplementary educational courseware stands up to university grading rigors, EngiPrepHub mandates that every public document undergoes comprehensive scholastic peer-review. This oversight protects young engineering students from common conceptual traps, algebraic errors, incorrect matrix operations, and obsolete programming methodologies.

2. The Quality Assurance Pipeline

Our verification protocol is a multi-tier pipeline designed to verify and optimize clarity:

Phase 01: Specialized Editorial Draft

Department specialists compile courseware drafts, mapping them to standard textbook and official JNTU-K syllabus structures. They consult sessional PYQs and toppers notebooks to maximize concept utility.

Phase 02: Verification by Faculty Review Board

The draft is submitted to our Quality Review Board—composed of retired lecturers, engineering PhDs, and senior academic toppers. They audit mathematical steps, check physical constants, and confirm that the proofs conform to standard evaluation criteria.

Phase 03: technical Enrichment & UI Formatting

UI developers and technical editors enhance the verified drafts with interactive simulators, code compilers, or responsive SVG visual aids, preparing the package for public staging.

3. Quality Review Board Qualification Matrix

Members of the EngiPrepHub Academic Review Board must satisfy strict credentials criteria to supervise course curriculum content. Qualification criteria include:

  • Lecturer Status: Must carry a registered Ph.D. or Master of Technology degree from an accredited institution with a minimum of 5 years of active engineering department instructions.
  • Topper Status: Student contributors must rank in the top 1% of their cumulative JNTUK university GPA boards and hold formal recommendations from departmental teachers.
  • Continuous Review: All members participate in annual pedagogical quality standard drills to align with modern instructional and AdSense educational criteria.

4. Conflict Interest Clearances

Our peer review processes operate without commercial or evaluation biases. Reviewers are forbidden from auditing materials authored by immediate relatives or students under their personal grading jurisdiction. This ensures first-class academic integrity and objective oversight across all documents.
