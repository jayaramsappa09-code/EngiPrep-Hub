Syllabus Guide: Decoding JNTUK Official Notification Pipelines

JNTU Kakinada releases millions of circulars annually, covering schedules, exam fee deadlines, credit structures, and results. Review this exhaustive operations registry compiled by our team of staff representatives to know how and when notifications are updated.

📅 JNTUK Academic Calendar Structure

Under standard R23 guidelines, JNTU Kakinada announces an official, unitary calendar for both Phase-1 and Phase-2 cohorts at the beginning of each academic term. A standard semester spans exactly 20-22 weeks, structured into high-priority cycles:

  • Instruction Phase I: Spans 8 continuous calendar weeks of core class lectures, assignments, and workshops.
  • Midterm Exam I: Slated during the 9th week, covering Unit-1 and Unit-2 descriptive/quiz structures.
  • Instruction Phase II: Runs 8 subsequent calendar weeks targeting the remaining Unit-3, Unit-4, and Unit-5 schedules.
  • Midterm Exam II: Slated during the 18th week, examining practical and theoretical structures.
  • Practical/Lab Practicals SEE: Slated during the 19th-20th week of college sessions.
  • Theory Semester End SEE: Slated during the 21st-22nd week across designated regional centers.

💰 Exam Registration & Fee Notification Workflows

JNTUK requires mandatory exam fee registrations before any student can sit for Semester End Examinations (SEE). Missing circular deadlines results in severe penalties:

  • Without Late Fee: Offered for about 10-14 days on the JNTUK portal after the circular triggers (Standard Fee: Rs. 770/-).
  • With Late Fee of Rs. 100/-: Extended for 2 to 3 days post original deadlines.
  • With Late Fee of Rs. 1000/-: Extended for 2 days directly preceding examinations.
  • With Late Fee of Rs. 5000/-: Emergency registration path, subject to executive board approval on site.

Insight Center: Recounting (RC) & Revaluation (RV)

If your final grading on a specific subject sheet (such as Data Structures or Applied Chemistry) does not match your evaluation targets, JNTUK provides highly specific appeal models:

1. Recounting Processes (RC)

Recounting involves a clerical review of the answer script. A dedicated clerk checks whether all pages are evaluated and checks the summation math of individual page scores. No recalculation of step value is performed. Fee is minor (Rs. 100/- per subject page).

2. Revaluation Processes (RV)

Revaluation triggers a complete re-grading from scratch. A completely new examiner grades the scanned paper script. If the revised score varies by more than 15% from the original score, the new score replaces the old one. Fee is standard (Rs. 1000/- per subject page).
