Academic Correction & Version Maintenance Policy

Last updated & audited: June 2026

1. Commitment to Scientific and Mathematical Correctness

Our commitment to rigorous scientific correctness is absolute. Since JNTU Kakinada engineering scholars rely on our supplementary guides, derivation steps, and code blocks to secure high CGPA awards, even minor typos can introduce confusion during sessional examinations. We strive to maintain zero errors across our repository and encourage active corrective feedback from students and faculty alike.

2. Feedback and Typo Reporting Workflows

If you identify an algebraic error, an incorrect sign polarity, a mismatched definition, or a programming syntax bug, you can report it via these official channels:

  • Dynamic Form: Access our Official Contact Portal, set your purpose selection as "Correct an Error/Typo in Lecture notes", and paste the exact URL with details.
  • Direct Support Email: Compose an email outlining the correction parameters and send it to our administration desk: engiprephub@gmail.com.

3. Multi-tier Corrections SLA (Service Level Agreement)

We honor student reports with rapid response SLAs:

  • Typo & Sign Corrections: Fixed and content updated live on our platform in < 24 Hours.
  • Derivation steps or code refactoring: Audited by faculty and updated in < 48 Hours.
  • Missing curriculum concepts or pyq requests: Handled and integrated inside of 3-5 Days.

4. Systematic Change Logs

Major edits and syllabus corrections are recorded systematically in our content version logs (e.g. tracking additions from Version 2.4 to 2.5). This maintain-history helps educators and examiners track the dynamic evolution of our resources and verifies that student experiences remain supported and up-to-date.
