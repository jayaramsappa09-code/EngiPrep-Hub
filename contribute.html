Strict Content Acceptance Criteria

To maintain Google AdSense and E-E-A-T academic authority, all submissions are filtered through an automated structural and manual university-chair review process:

  • Zero Plagiarism: PDFs or screenshots scanned directly from proprietary retail books or competitor platforms are instantly rejected. Content must be your own notes, drawings, or explanations.
  • High Legibility: Handwritten notes must be digitized using high-yield cam scanners or tablet applications with high-contrast monochrome layouts. Blurred, yellowed, or dark-shadow scan pages are filtered.
  • Standard Notations: Math derivations and physics formula constants must correspond with local board (JNTUK) textbook recommendations.

Contributor Checklist Guidelines

1. Mathematics Papers: Must show clear intermediate steps. Do not skip limit evaluation reductions or row transformations.

2. Electrical Engineering: Node circuits must label passive direction currents and references.

3. Programming Lab sheets: Must specify GCC/Clang optimization parameters, recursive stack traces, and complete dry-run arrays tables.

4. Engineering Graphics: Include clean snapshots of mechanical orthographic projections, clearly indicating projection planes (HP/VP) and alignment rules.
