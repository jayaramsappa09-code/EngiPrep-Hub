Frequently Academic Questions (FAQs)

Q.1 How are marks converted into JNTUK R23 letter grades?

JNTUK R23 represents an absolute grading frame. The continuous internal evaluation (CIE - 30M) and semester end evaluation (SEE - 70M) scores are added to retrieve a total score out of 100. Any score of 90% or higher maps to 'O' (Outstanding, 10 grade points), while scores below 40% will trigger an 'F' (Fail) grade. Refer to the table on the right panel for the complete coordinate matrix.

Q.2 What is condonation in JNTUK attendance policy?

If a student fails to meet the 75% attendance threshold but holds equal or greater than 65% total semester attendance, they can apply for Condonation of shortage of attendance by submitting valid proof of medical urgency or academic representation along with the university-prescribed payment of a condonation fee. If attendance is under 65%, condonation is impossible, and the student is detained.

Q.3 Can I recover my CGPA if I suffer a backlog?

Yes. On clearing the backlog in supplementary examinations, the newly secured letter grade replaces the failing 'F' grade point in subsequent SGPA and CGPA math calculations. To secure an ultimate 9.0 CGPA, focus heavily on maximizing high-weightage core theory papers in intermediate semesters.

R23 Regulation Brief

1. Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) Integration

The JNTUK R23 system monitors student performance inside a single semester using SGPA. The SGPA represents the ratio of sum of product of credits and grade points secured across all registered subjects to the sum of total registered credits of that semester.

SGPA (Si) = Σ(Ci x Gi) / ΣCi

Where Ci denotes the credits allocated for subject i, and Gi denotes the corresponding grade points scored. If you secure backlogs, credits are registered but Gi becomes 0.

2. Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) Math

The aggregate progress across all studied semesters is compiled into the CGPA score. The formula divides total earned grade point sums over all semesters by total credits registered up to that academic semester point.

CGPA = Σ(C_j x S_j) / ΣC_j

Maintaining constant progress is key because credits are cumulative: scoring low points in large 3-credit or 4-credit papers heavily lowers your overall CGPA, making recovery exceptionally taxing later in the program.

3. JNTUK Class Division Classifications

Graduation degree certificates are awarded distinct class boundaries upon completion of B.Tech requirements based on the final achieved CGPA:

  • CGPA ≥ 7.75: Awarded First Class with Distinction (conducted within the standard 4-year cycle).
  • 6.5 ≤ CGPA < 7.75: Awarded First Class.
  • 5.5 ≤ CGPA < 6.5: Awarded Second Class.
  • 5.0 ≤ CGPA < 5.5: Awarded Pass Class.

4. Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) details

In the JNTUK R23 guidelines, CIE is allocated 30 marks out of 100. This is split into Midterm exams (20 Marks), class assessment quiz/assignments (5 Marks), and active attendance/viva indicators (5 Marks). A minimum of 40% combined target in both CIE +SEE is required to clear.

Since SEE is evaluated externally, securing high CIE marks (at least 25 out of 30) gives you a substantial cushion. It ensures you only need 15-20 marks in the external SEE to clear, avoiding supplementary exam delays.
